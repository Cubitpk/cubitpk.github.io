Scaling from Blank Pouches + Roll Labels to Custom Printed Stand-Up Pouches (US Brand Guide)

Food, coffee, supplement, pet-treat, and skincare brands usually scale packaging in two clean stages:

  1. Stage 1 — Waterproof BOPP Roll Labels (From 500 Labels): Pairing stock jars, bottles, or blank bags with laminated white, clear, or metallic BOPP roll labels protects artwork against water, fridge condensation, and essential oils. Machine-applied rolls use a 3" core and unwind direction #1–#8. Calculate exact wrap width and unwind chart specs with CubitPack's Label Size & Unwind Calculator and see how commercial rolls compare with office label sheets at OnlineLabels & Avery Alternatives.
  2. Stage 2 — Custom Printed Stand-Up Pouches & Mylar Bags: Eliminates hand-labeling labor while adding full-surface CMYK/matte/soft-touch branding, resealable zippers, tear notches, and high-barrier foil or metalized films. Estimate standard 2 oz to 5 lb pouch dimensions with the Stand-Up Pouch Size Calculator and review US supplier specs at Custom Mylar Bag & Pouch Suppliers USA.
