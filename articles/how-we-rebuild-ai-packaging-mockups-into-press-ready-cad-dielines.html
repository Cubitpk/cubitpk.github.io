How to Turn Google Gemini, ChatGPT & Midjourney Packaging Mockups into Press-Ready CAD Dielines

Generative AI models like Google Gemini, ChatGPT (GPT-Image), Claude, Midjourney, and Canva AI have changed how founders prototype custom boxes, roll labels, and stand-up pouches. In 30 seconds you can visualize soft-touch matte black cardstock, gold hot foil stamping, and embossed typography.

The catch shows up the moment you send that PNG to a commercial offset or flexographic press.

Why Raw AI Images Cannot Go Straight to Press

  1. 3D Perspective vs. Flat 2D CAD Dielines: Presses print flat sheets with exact fold allowances, glue tabs, tuck flaps, dust flaps, and 0.125 in (3 mm) bleed past every cut line.
  2. 72 DPI RGB Raster vs. 300+ DPI CMYK / Pantone Spot Ink: AI renders bake specular highlights and shadows into RGB pixels. Printing baked-in foil highlights in CMYK produces muddy yellow-brown ink instead of metallic foil.
  3. Rasterized Micro-Text & Barcodes: Ingredient lists, net-weight statements, and UPC/EAN barcodes generated by diffusion or multimodal models have soft pixel edges that fail retail scanner verification.
  4. Missing Vector Spot Masks: Hot foil stamping, blind embossing, debossing, and spot UV each require an isolated 100% K vector spot layer aligned in register with the print plate.
