If you have ever needed a press-ready packaging dieline for Illustrator, Affinity Designer, or Inkscape, almost every "free dieline template" site puts up an account wall, watermarks the file, or locks custom millimeter/inch dimensions behind a paid subscription.

In structural packaging, standard folding cartons and corrugated mailers follow ECMA and FEFCO geometry rules parameterized by three inside dimensions (L × W × H) plus board thickness (t):

  • FEFCO 0427 (Corrugated Mailer Box): Roll-over double side walls require 2t–3t fold allowances so locking tabs seat cleanly into bottom slots without bowing.
  • Straight Tuck End (STE) & Reverse Tuck End (RTE): Glue flap, four main body panels (L, W, L, W), dust flaps, and friction/slit-lock tuck flaps.
  • Auto-Lock Bottom (Crash-Lock) & 1-2-3 Snap-Lock Bottom: Pre-glued or interlocking bottom flaps engineered so heavier jars and candles stay supported.
