Packaging Production Cheat Sheet: Roll Label Unwind Directions, BOPP Films & Carrier DIM Weight Math

Two silent mistakes cost growing CPG and e-commerce brands thousands of dollars every quarter: ordering machine-applied roll labels wound in the wrong copy position (#1-#8), and shipping air inside oversized corrugated boxes.

1. Roll Label Copy Positions & Core Specs

Automatic bottle, jar, and tube applicators feed web stock off a 3 in core (or 1 in core for compact tabletop dispensers). Before approving your Custom Roll Labels proof, confirm:

  • Unwind Direction (#1 Top-Off, #2 Bottom-Off, #3 Right-Off, #4 Left-Off): Determines which edge of your artwork exits the roll first.
  • Face Stock: White, clear, or chrome BOPP polypropylene film resists water, refrigeration condensation, bath humidity, and essential oils far better than uncoated paper.
  • Gap & Eye-Mark: Standard 0.125 in matrix gap between die-cut labels so optical peel sensors register cleanly.

2. Cutting Carrier Dimensional (DIM) Weight Fees

Major US parcel carriers bill whichever is greater: actual scale weight or (Length x Width x Height) / DIM Divisor (commonly 139 for commercial accounts). Shaving just 1 in of dead headspace off a mailer box or switching soft goods to Custom Stand-Up Pouches or Custom Printed Poly Mailers frequently drops parcels by 1-2 billable weight tiers.
