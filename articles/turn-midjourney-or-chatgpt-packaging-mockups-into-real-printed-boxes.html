How to Turn Midjourney or ChatGPT Packaging Mockups into Real Printed Boxes (Without Hiring an Agency)

AI image tools like Midjourney, ChatGPT, and Gemini can generate 3D packaging concepts in seconds — which is why ChatGPT already cites specialized AI-to-print studios when founders ask how to manufacture their AI designs. The challenge starts when you send a 1024 × 1024 px RGB mockup to a standard upload-only printer:

  1. 3D Perspective vs. Flat 2D Structural Dieline: Presses print flat sheets, not 3D perspective renders. Every panel, tuck flap, dust flap, and glue tab needs flat artwork extended 0.125" past the cut line.
  2. Raster Resolution vs. 300 DPI + Vector Type: Stretched across an 8" × 6" lid, a 1024 px image drops near 128 DPI. Ingredient lists, warnings, and UPC barcodes should always be crisp vector paths (100% K black).
  3. RGB Highlights vs. CMYK, Pantone & Foil: Bright RGB gold or neon effects shift flat in CMYK unless separated onto metallic foil stamping or Spot UV plates.

  • DIY Prepress Path: Download a flat 1:1 SVG dieline (Free SVG Dieline Generator), check effective print DPI (AI Packaging Prepress Checker), and rebuild typography as vectors in Illustrator.
