Most small ecommerce founders hit the same wall when moving from plain brown cartons to custom printed boxes: browser 3D calculators look simple at 5,000 units, then jump sharply on starter runs once custom tooling, inside printing, or parcel/LTL zone freight gets added at checkout.

If you are ordering your first custom packaging run in the US, these five specs determine your actual landed unit cost:

  1. Inside vs. Outside Dimensions: Box dielines are always engineered to inside dimensions (Length × Width × Height). Corrugated E-flute (~1/16" wall) folds double on the side walls of a roll-end mailer, adding roughly 3/8" to outside width. Check carrier dimensional weight (L × W × H ÷ 139) before locking dimensions with a free calculator like CubitPack's Dimensional Weight Calculator.
