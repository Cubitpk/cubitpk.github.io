What Mylar Bag Thickness Do You Need: 3.5 Mil, 5 Mil or 7 Mil?

Pick 3.5 to 4 mil film for light retail snacks and herbs, 5 mil for coffee, bath salts and dry pantry staples, and 7 mil when sharp edges or multi-pound bulk fills demand maximum puncture protection.
