Single-wall vs double-wall cardboard boxes: when do you need 48 ECT heavy-duty board?

Use single-wall 32 ECT B-flute or C-flute [custom cardboard boxes](/custom-cardboard-boxes) for individual shipments up to 30-40 lb. Step up to double-wall 48 ECT corrugated board (EB-flute for printed retail kits up to 65 lb, or BC-flute for master cartons up to 85 lb) whenever payloads exceed 40 lb, hold fragile glass, or stack on winter pallets.
