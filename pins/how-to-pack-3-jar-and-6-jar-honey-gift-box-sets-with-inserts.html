How Do You Pack a 3-Jar or 6-Jar Honey Gift Box Set So No Jar Breaks?

Pack 3-jar and 6-jar [custom honey boxes](/custom-honey-boxes) with at least 0.50 inch solid insert bridges between glass pockets, 0.50 to 0.75 inch outer perimeter cushions, and opposite 0.75 inch finger-pull cutouts. Pair heavy 8 oz sets with EPE foam or corrugated dividers, and mail rigid boxes inside fitted 32 ECT shippers.
