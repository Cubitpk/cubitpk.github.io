What cardstock weight and finish is best for business thank you cards and packaging inserts?

Choose 16pt (350 GSM) C1S paperboard - coated on the front for vibrant photography and uncoated on the back for smudge-free pen notes - for everyday [custom thank you cards](/custom-thank-you-cards). Upgrade to 18pt (400 GSM) soft-touch card with foil for luxury boxes, or 14pt (300 GSM) uncoated stock for fully handwritten notes.
